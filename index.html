Good software should feel invisible — it just works, and gets out of the way.

I'm Weihao Wu, a systems developer who lives between the worlds of hardware and software. I've built web maps that guide accessibility surveys, FPGA-based digital systems, and small tools that make other tools friendlier.

Right now I'm exploring where AI agents, GIS and the open web meet — and trying to keep everything I ship simple enough to explain to a friend. The receipts live on GitHub.
